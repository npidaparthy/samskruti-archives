avidyopādhiko jīvo māyopādhika īśvaraḥ
māyāvidyāguṇātītaṃ brahma vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The jīva is conditioned by the limiting adjunct of nescience; Īśvara is conditioned by the adjunct of the cosmic power. Brahman transcends māyā, avidyā, and the guṇas altogether. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 31
