sākāraṃ ca nirākāraṃ saguṇaṃ cāguṇātmakam
tattvaṃ tadbrahma paramamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

That Supreme Reality, Brahman, manifests both as having form and as formless, as having attributes and as attributeless in its true nature. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 32
