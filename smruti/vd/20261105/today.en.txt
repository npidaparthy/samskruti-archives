dehādipañcakośasthā yā sphūrtiranubhūyate
sā sphūrtirātmā naivānyaditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The luminous awareness experienced within the five sheaths, beginning with the body, is the Self alone, and nothing else. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 52
