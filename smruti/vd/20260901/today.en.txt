dhyeyādhyeyapadārthau dvau dhīsamādhyasamādhidau
dhyātavyaṃ brahma naivānyaditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

What is worth meditating on and what is not are two categories — one giving the mind settledness, the other agitation. Brahman alone is worth meditating on, nothing else. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 10
