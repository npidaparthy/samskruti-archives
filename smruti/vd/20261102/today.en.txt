vṛthākriyā vṛthālāpānvṛthāvādānmanorathān
tyaktvaikaṃ brahma vijñeyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Abandoning useless actions, useless chatter, useless disputation, and idle desires, one should strive to know the one Brahman. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 14
