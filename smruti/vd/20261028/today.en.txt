na vidvāṃsaṃ tapeccittaṃ karaṇe'karaṇe dhruvam
sarvamātmeti vijñānāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Owing to the firm knowledge that everything is the Self, the wise person's mind certainly does not suffer distress whether engaged in action or in inaction. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 91
