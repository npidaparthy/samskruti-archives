jñānakarmapadārthau dvau vastukartrātmatantrakau
jñānānmokṣo na karmabhya iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Knowledge and action are two categories — one dependent on the object/reality, the other on the doer's will. Liberation comes from knowledge alone, not from actions. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 7
