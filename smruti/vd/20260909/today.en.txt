yadastyādau yadastyante yanmadhye bhāti tatsvayam
brahmaivaikamidaṃ satyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

That which exists at the beginning, that which exists at the end, and that which shines by itself in between — all this is Brahman alone. This one alone is reality. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 38
