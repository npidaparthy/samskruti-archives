vṛthā śramo'yaṃ viduṣāṃ vṛthāyaṃ karmiṇāṃ śramaḥ
yadi na brahmavijñānamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

If knowledge of Brahman does not result, then the effort of scholars is futile, and the effort of ritual-performers is futile too. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 22
