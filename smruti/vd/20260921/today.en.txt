daśamasya parijñāne nāyāso'sti yathā tathā
svasya brahmātmavijñāna iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Just as there is no exertion in recognising the presence of the tenth man, there is likewise no exertion in knowing one's own self as Brahman. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 75
