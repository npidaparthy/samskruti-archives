dehādipañcakośasthā yā sattā pratibhāsate
sā sattātmā na sandeha iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The existence that shines within the five sheaths, beginning with the body, is the Self itself; there is no doubt about this. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 51
