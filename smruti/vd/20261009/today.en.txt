sarvātmanā sthitaṃ brahma sarvaṃ brahmātmanā sthitam
na kāryaṃ kāraṇādbhinnamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Brahman exists as the essence of everything; everything exists having Brahman as its essence. The effect is never different from its cause. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 34
