saccidānandasatyatve mithyātve nāmarūpayoḥ
vijñāte kimidaṃ jñeyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Once one knows that existence-consciousness-bliss is real and that name and form are unreal, what more remains to be known? This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 87
