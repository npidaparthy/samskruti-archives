dvijatvaṃ vidhyanuṣṭhānādvipratvaṃ veda pāṭhataḥ
brāhmaṇyaṃ brahmavijñānāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

From performing enjoined rites comes the status of the twice-born; from studying the Vedas comes the status of a vipra; but true brahmana-hood comes only from knowledge of Brahman. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 33
