jīvo brahmātmanā jñeyo jñeyaṃ jīvātmanā param
muktistadaikyavijñānānāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The individual is to be known as essentially Brahman, and the Supreme is to be known as the individual's very self. Liberation comes from knowledge of this very unity. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 16
