supterutthāya suptyantaṃ brahmaikaṃ pravicintyatām
nātidūre nṛṇāṃ mṛtyuriti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

From waking to sleeping again, one should continuously contemplate the one Brahman. For human beings, death is never far away. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 79
