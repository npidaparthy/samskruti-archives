abhedadarśanaṃ mokṣaḥ saṃsāro bhedadarśanam
sarvavedāntasiddhānta iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The vision of non-difference is liberation; the vision of difference is worldly bondage. This is the settled conclusion of the entire Vedanta. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 71
