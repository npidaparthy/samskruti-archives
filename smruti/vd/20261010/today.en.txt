parokṣātsaphalaṃ karma jñānaṃ pratyakṣasatphalam
jñānamevābhyasettasmāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Ritual action bears its fruit indirectly, unseen; knowledge bears real fruit directly, immediately seen. Therefore one should practise knowledge alone. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 21
