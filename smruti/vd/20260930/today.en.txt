dhanairvā dhanadaiḥ putrairdārāgārasahodaraiḥ
dhruvaṃ prāṇaharairduḥkhamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Wealth, the means of acquiring it, sons, wife, house, and siblings — all these drain one's vitality and certainly leave only sorrow behind. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 78
