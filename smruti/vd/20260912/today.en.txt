sarvātmatvātparaṃ brahma śroturātmatayā sthitam
nāyāsastattvavijñaptyāviti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Owing to its being the Self of all, the Supreme Brahman exists as the very self of the listener. There is no exertion involved in realising this truth. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 17
