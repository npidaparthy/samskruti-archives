sthito brahmātmanā jīvo brahma jīvātmanā sthitam
iti sampaśyatāṃ muktiriti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The individual exists in essence as Brahman itself; Brahman exists as the individual's very self. For those who see this clearly, liberation follows. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 15
