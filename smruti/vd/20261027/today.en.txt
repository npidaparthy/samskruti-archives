upekṣyaupādhikāndoṣāngṛhyante viṣayā yathā
upekṣya dṛśyaṃ tadbrahma iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Just as we accept objects while disregarding their incidental faults, so too we must accept Brahman while disregarding the seen world. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 76
