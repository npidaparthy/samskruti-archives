svakarmapāśavaśagaḥ prājño'nyo vā jano dhruvam
prājñaḥ sukhaṃ nayetkālamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Whether one is wise or an ordinary person, both are certainly subject to the bonds of their own past karma. The wise, however, pass their time happily. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 90
