prāṇo nāhamahaṃ devaḥ prāṇasākṣīti niścayāt
kṣutpipāsopaśāntiḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The life-force is not I; I am self-luminous, the witness of the life-force — through this firm conviction, the afflictions of hunger and thirst are stilled. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 44
