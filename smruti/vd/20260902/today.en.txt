yogino bhogino vāpi tyāgino rāgiṇo'pi ca
jñānānmokṣo na sandeha iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Whether one is a yogi or a pleasure-seeker, a renunciant or one still attached — liberation comes from knowledge alone, there is no doubt about it. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 11
