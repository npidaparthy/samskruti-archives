navābhāsānnava jñātvā navopādhīnnavātmanā ।
mithyā jñātvāvaśiṣṭe tu maunaṃ vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Having known the nine appearances and the nine limiting adjuncts to be unreal, not the Self — in what remains, only silence prevails. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 83
