na jīvabrahmaṇorbhedo nāmnā rūpeṇa vidyate
nāmno rūpasya mithyātvāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

No difference between the individual and Brahman can be established through name and form — because name and form are themselves unreal. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 65
