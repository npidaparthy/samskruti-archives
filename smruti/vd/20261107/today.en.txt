nāhaṃ sarvamahaṃ sarvaṃ mama sarvamiti sphuṭam
jñāte tattve kuto duḥkhamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

I am none of this creation; yet I am all; and all is mine — this is clear. Once this truth is known, whence could sorrow ever come? This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 50
