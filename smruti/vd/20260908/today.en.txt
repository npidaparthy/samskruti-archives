karmāṇi cittaśuddhyarthamaikāgryārthamupāsanā
mokṣārthaṃ brahmavijñānamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Rituals are for purifying the mind; meditation is for one-pointedness of mind; knowledge of Brahman is for liberation. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 26
