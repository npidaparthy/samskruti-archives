alaṃ yāgairalaṃ yogairalaṃ bhogairalaṃ dhanaiḥ
parasminbrahmaṇi jñāta iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Once the Supreme Brahman is known, there is no more need for sacrifices, no more need for yogic practices, no more need for pleasures, no more need for wealth. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 23
