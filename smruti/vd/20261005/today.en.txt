dehādikośagā sphūrtiryā sā vyomādibhūtagā
mānābhāvānna tadbheda iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The luminous awareness within the sheaths and that within the elements are one and the same. Since no difference can be established, there is none. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 58
