pratilomānulomābhyāṃ viśvāropāpavādayoḥ
cintane śiṣyate tattvamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Through forward-order contemplation of how the universe is superimposed, and through reverse-order negation of that same superimposition, what remains is the underlying truth alone. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 85
