jñānājñānapadārthau dvāvātmano bandhamuktidau
jñānānmuktirnibandho'nyaditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Knowledge and ignorance are two categories — giving the self liberation and bondage respectively. From knowledge comes liberation; the other, ignorance, is bondage. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 3
