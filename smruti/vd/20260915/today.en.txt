nāhaṃ māyā na tatkāryaṃ tatsākṣī paramo'smyaham
iti niḥsaṃśayajñānānānmuktirvedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

I am not māyā, nor am I its effect; I am its witness, I am the Supreme itself. Liberation comes from this doubt-free knowledge. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 49
