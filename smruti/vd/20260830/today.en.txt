na nāmarūpe niyate sarvatra vyabhicārataḥ ।
anāmarūpaḥ sarvaḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Name and form are never fixed anywhere, being constantly inconsistent. Therefore everything is, in truth, beyond name and form. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 61
