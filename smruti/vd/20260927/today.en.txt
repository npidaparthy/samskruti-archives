nājñānaṃ syāmahaṃ devo'jñānasākṣīti niścayāt
sarvānarthanivṛttiḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

I am not ignorance itself; I am self-luminous, the witness even of ignorance — through this firm conviction, freedom from all misfortune follows. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 47
