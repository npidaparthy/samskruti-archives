alaṃ vedairalaṃ śāstrairalaṃ smṛtipurāṇakaiḥ
paramātmani vijñāta iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

Once the Supreme Self is known, there is no more need for the Vedas, no more need for the treatises, no more need for the Smritis and Puranas. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 24
