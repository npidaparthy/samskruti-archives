nāmarūpābhimānaḥ syātsaṃsāraḥ sarvadehinām
saccidānandadṛṣṭiḥ syānmuktirvedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

For all embodied beings, identification with name and form is worldly bondage; the vision of existence-consciousness-bliss is liberation. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 86
