jñātṛjñeyapadārthau dvau bhāsyabhāsakalakṣaṇau
jñātā brahma jagajjñeyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ

The knower and the known are two categories — marked by the nature of illuminator and illuminated. The knower is Brahman itself; the universe is what is known. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— వేదాన్తడిణ్డిమః · శ్లో. 4
