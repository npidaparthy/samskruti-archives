caturbhiḥ śrīkaṇṭhaiḥ śivayuvatibhiḥ pañcabhirapi
prabhinnābhiḥ śambhōrnavabhirapi mūlaprakṛtibhiḥ
catuścatvāriṃśadvasudalakalāśratrivalaya-
trirēkhābhiḥ sārdhaṃ tava śaraṇakōṇāḥ pariṇatāḥ

All fourteen branches of learning and sixty-four arts emerged from Devi's form.
