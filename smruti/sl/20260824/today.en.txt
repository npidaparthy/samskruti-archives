japō jalpaḥ śilpaṃ sakalamapi mudrāviracanā
gatiḥ prādakṣiṇyakramaṇamaśanādyāhutividhiḥ
praṇāmaḥ saṃvēśaḥ sukhamakhilamātmārpaṇadṛśā
saparyāparyāyastava bhavatu yanmē vilasitam

The poet confesses he cannot describe Devi's eyes and simply prays for a single glance.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 27
