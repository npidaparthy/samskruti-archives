arālaiḥ svābhāvyādalikalabhasaśrībhiralakaiḥ
parītaṃ tē vaktraṃ parihasati paṅkēruharucim
darasmērē yasmindaśanarucikiñjalkarucirē
sugandhau mādyanti smaradahanacakṣurmadhulihaḥ

Devi's eyebrows rival Kāmadeva's bow; her glance itself is the flower arrow.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 45
