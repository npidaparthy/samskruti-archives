nisargakṣīṇasya stanataṭabharēṇa klamajuṣaḥ
namanmūrtērnārītilaka śanakaistruṭyata iva
ciraṃ tē madhyasya truṭitataṭinītīrataruṇā
samāvasthāsthēmnō bhavatu kuśalaṃ śailatanayē

Devi is the Saraswati of all learning — the mother who carries the nectar of wisdom.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 79
