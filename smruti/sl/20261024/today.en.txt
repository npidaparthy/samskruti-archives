harakrōdhajvālāvalibhiravalīḍhēna vapuṣā
gabhīrē tē nābhīsarasi kṛtasaṅgō manasijaḥ
samuttasthau tasmādacalatanayē dhūmalatikā
janastāṃ jānītē tava janani rōmāvaliriti

Devi's lāsya (gentle dance) is the source of all beauty; her dance brings joy to the worlds.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 76
