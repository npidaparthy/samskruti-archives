mṛṣā kṛtvā gōtraskhalanamatha vailakṣyanamitaṃ
lalāṭē bhartāraṃ caraṇakamalē tāḍayati tē
cirādantaḥśalyaṃ dahanakṛtamunmūlitavatā
tulākōṭikvāṇaiḥ kilikilitamīśānaripuṇā

Devi is the four levels of speech — Parā, Paśyantī, Madhyamā, Vaikharī — speech in all its forms.
