dhunōtu dhvāntaṃ nastulitadalitēndīvaravanaṃ
ghanasnigdhaślakṣṇaṃ cikuranikurumbaṃ tava śivē
yadīyaṃ saurabhyaṃ sahajamupalabdhuṃ sumanasaḥ
vasantyasminmanyē valamathanavāṭīviṭapinām

Devi's dark curling locks on her forehead arch like a bow, doubling her beauty.
