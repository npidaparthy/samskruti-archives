tavādhārē mūlē saha samayayā lāsyaparayā
navātmānaṃ manyē navarasamahātāṇḍavanaṭam
ubhābhyāmētābhyāmudayavidhimuddiśya dayayā
sanāthābhyāṃ jajñē janakajananīmajjagadidam

The close of Ānanda Laharī — the poet bows at Devi's feet before moving to the song of her beauty.
