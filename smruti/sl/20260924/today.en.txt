tava svādhiṣṭhānē hutavahamadhiṣṭhāya nirataṃ
tamīḍē saṃvartaṃ janani mahatīṃ tāṃ ca samayām
yadālōkē lōkāndahati mahati krōdhakalitē
dayārdrā yā dṛṣṭiḥ śiśiramupacāraṃ racayati

Shiva himself is the couch on which Devi reclines — he supports her in complete submission.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 39
