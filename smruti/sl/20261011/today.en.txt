kucau sadyaḥ svidyattaṭaghaṭitakūrpāsabhidurau
kaṣantau dōrmūlē kanakakalaśābhau kalayatā
tava trātuṃ bhaṅgādalamiti valagnaṃ tanubhuvā
tridhā naddhaṃ dēvi trivali lavalīvallibhiriva

Devi is the power of learning; the poet blessed by her speaks with eloquence and grace.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 80
