arālaṃ tē pālīyugalamagarājanyatanayē
na kēṣāmādhattē kusumaśarakōdaṇḍakutukam
tiraścīnō yatra śravaṇapathamullaṅghya vilasan
apāṅgavyāsaṅgō diśati śarasandhānadhiṣaṇām

Devi's hips are wide as a lotus — a symbol of infinite abundance and motherhood.
