samunmīlatsaṃvitkamalamakarandaikarasikaṃ
bhajē haṃsadvandvaṃ kimapi mahatāṃ mānasacaram
yadālāpādaṣṭādaśaguṇitavidyāpariṇatiḥ
yadādattē dōṣādguṇamakhilamadbhyaḥ paya iva

Devi dwells on Maṇidvīpa, a jeweled island floating in an ocean of ambrosia.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 38
