karāgrēṇa spṛṣṭaṃ tuhinagiriṇā vatsalatayā
girīśēnōdastaṃ muhuradharapānākulatayā
karagrāhyaṃ śambhōrmukhamukuravṛntaṃ girisutē
kathaṅkāraṃ brūmastava cubukamaupamyarahitam

Devi's sidelong glance overflows with compassion; those who receive it are purified across all births.
