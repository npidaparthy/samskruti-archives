jagatsūtē dhātā hariravati rudraḥ kṣapayatē
tiraskurvannētatsvamapi vapurīśastirayati
sadāpūrvaḥ sarvaṃ tadidamanugṛhṇāti ca śivaḥ
tavājñāmālambya  kṣaṇacalitayōrbhrūlatikayōḥ

Devi bears the crescent moon on her forehead; its light illuminates and cools all the worlds.
