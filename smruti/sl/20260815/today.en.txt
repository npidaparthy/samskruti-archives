mukhaṃ binduṃ kṛtvā kucayugamadhastasya tadadhaḥ
harārdhaṃ dhyāyēdyō haramahiṣi tē manmathakalām
sa sadyaḥ saṅkṣōbhaṃ nayati vanitā ityatilaghu
trilōkīmapyāśu bhramayati ravīndustanayugām

Devi is the consciousness beyond the five elements — the source of all nature.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 19
