padanyāsakrīḍāparicayamivārabdhumanasaḥ
skhalantastē khēlaṃ bhavanakalahaṃsā na jahati
atastēṣāṃ śikṣāṃ subhagamaṇimañjīraraṇita-
cchalādācakṣāṇaṃ caraṇakamalaṃ cārucaritē

Devi is the embodiment of compassion — her mercy flows equally on all beings without partiality.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 91
