kirantīmaṅgēbhyaḥ kiraṇanikurumbāmṛtarasaṃ
hṛdi tvāmādhattē himakaraśilāmūrtimiva yaḥ
sa sarpāṇāṃ darpaṃ śamayati śakuntādhipa iva
jvarapluṣṭāndṛṣṭyā sukhayati sudhādhārasirayā

Devi is the goddess of speech — from the tip of her tongue flow the words of the Vedas.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 20
