tvadanyaḥ pāṇibhyāmabhayavaradō daivatagaṇaḥ
tvamēkā naivāsi prakaṭitavarābhītyabhinayā
bhayāttrātuṃ dātuṃ phalamapi ca vāñchāsamadhikaṃ
śaraṇyē lōkānāṃ tava hi caraṇāvēva nipuṇau

The radiance from Devi's form illuminates the universe, surpassing even the sun.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 4
