sudhādhārāsāraiścaraṇayugalāntarvigalitaiḥ
prapañcaṃ siñcantī punarapi rasāmnāyamahasaḥ
avāpya svāṃ bhūmiṃ bhujaganibhamadhyuṣṭavalayaṃ
svamātmānaṃ kṛtvā svapiṣi kulakuṇḍē kuhariṇi

Devi's feet are the path to liberation; mere remembrance of her carries one across the ocean of existence.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 10
