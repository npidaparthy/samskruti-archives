lalāṭaṃ lāvaṇyadyutivimalamābhāti tava yat
dvitīyaṃ tanmanyē makuṭaghaṭitaṃ candraśakalam
viparyāsanyāsādubhayamapi sambhūya ca mithaḥ
sudhālēpasyūtiḥ pariṇamati rākāhimakaraḥ

Devi's eyes are like lotus petals — wide, dark, filled with compassion and love.
