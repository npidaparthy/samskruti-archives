dhanuḥ pauṣpaṃ maurvī madhukaramayī pañca viśikhāḥ
vasantaḥ sāmantō malayamarudāyōdhanarathaḥ
tathāpyēkaḥ sarvaṃ himagirisutē kāmapi kṛpām
apāṅgāttē labdhvā jagadidamanaṅgō vijayatē

Devi assumes various forms to fulfill devotees' desires; she grants liberation to those who seek it.
