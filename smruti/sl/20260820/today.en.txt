bhavāni tvaṃ dāsē mayi vitara dṛṣṭiṃ sakaruṇāṃ
iti stōtuṃ vāñchankathayati bhavāni tvamiti yaḥ
tadaiva tvaṃ tasmai diśasi nijasāyujyapadavīṃ
mukundabrahmēndrasphuṭamakuṭanīrājitapadām

Devi's love is a noose that binds devotees, yet that very bondage is liberation.
