amū tē vakṣōjāvamṛtarasamāṇikyakutupau
na sandēhaspandō nagapatipatākē manasi naḥ
pibantau tau yasmādaviditavadhūsaṅgarasikau
kumārāvadyāpi dviradavadanakrauñcadalanau

Devi's compassionate glance — one look from her transforms the devotee's life completely.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 73
