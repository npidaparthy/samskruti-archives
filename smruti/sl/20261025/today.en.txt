kavīnāṃ sandarbhastabakamakarandaikarasikaṃ
kaṭākṣavyākṣēpabhramarakalabhau karṇayugalam
amuñcantau dṛṣṭvā tava navarasāsvādataralau
asūyāsaṃsargādalikanayanaṃ kiñcidaruṇam

Devi's teeth gleam like pearls, making her smile a divine vision that enchants all beings.
