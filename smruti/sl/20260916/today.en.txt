taṭittvantaṃ śaktyā timiraparipanthisphuraṇayā
sphurannānāratnābharaṇapariṇaddhēndradhanuṣam
tava śyāmaṃ mēghaṃ kamapi maṇipūraikaśaraṇaṃ
niṣēvē varṣantaṃ haramihirataptaṃ tribhuvanam

Devi abides in eternal samādhi — her bliss is self-arising, complete, without external cause.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 40
