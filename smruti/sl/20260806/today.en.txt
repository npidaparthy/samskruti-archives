kalatraṃ vaidhātraṃ katikati bhajantē na kavayaḥ
śriyō dēvyāḥ kō vā na bhavati patiḥ kairapi dhanaiḥ
mahādēvaṃ hitvā tava sati satīnāmacaramē
kucābhyāmāsaṅgaḥ kuravakatarōrapyasulabhaḥ

Those who take refuge at Devi's feet and recite this stotra three times have all desires fulfilled.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 96
