vibhaktatraivarṇyaṃ vyatikaritalīlāñjanatayā
vibhāti tvannētratritayamidamīśānadayitē
punaḥ sraṣṭuṃ dēvāndruhiṇaharirudrānuparatān
rajaḥ sattvaṃ bibhrattama iti guṇānāṃ trayamiva

Devi's arms are tender as creepers — they are the arms of grace that embrace all devotees.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 53
