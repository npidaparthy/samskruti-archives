gatairmāṇikyatvaṃ gaganamaṇibhiḥ sāndraghaṭitaṃ
kirīṭaṃ tē haimaṃ himagirisutē kīrtayati yaḥ
sa nīḍēyacchāyācchuraṇaśabalaṃ candraśakalaṃ
dhanuḥ śaunāsīraṃ kimiti na nibadhnāti dhiṣaṇām

Devi's hair is blacker than the blue lotus, darker than clouds — unmatched dark beauty.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 42
