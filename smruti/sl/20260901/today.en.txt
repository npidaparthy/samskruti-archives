bhujāślēṣānnityaṃ puradamayituḥ kaṇṭakavatī
tava grīvā dhattē mukhakamalanālaśriyamiyam
svataḥ śvētā kālāgurubahulajambālamalinā
mṛṇālīlālityaṃ vahati yadadhō hāralatikā

In the crescent on Devi's forehead is ambrosia — that nectar is the life of all beings.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 68
