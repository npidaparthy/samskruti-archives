tanōtu kṣēmaṃ nastava vadanasaundaryalaharī-
parīvāhasrōtassaraṇiriva sīmantasaraṇiḥ
vahantī sindūraṃ prabalakabarībhāratimira-
dviṣāṃ bṛndairbandīkṛtamiva navīnārkakiraṇam

Devi's forehead glows like the full moon, illuminating all three worlds with its splendor.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 44
