dadānē dīnēbhyaḥ śriyamaniśamāśānusadṛśīṃ
amandaṃ saundaryaprakaramakarandaṃ vikirati
tavāsminmandārastabakasubhagē yātu caraṇē
nimajjanmajjīvaḥ karaṇacaraṇaḥ ṣaṭcaraṇatām

Devi is Prakṛti herself; the union of Prakṛti and Puruṣa is all of cosmic creation.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 90
