tanīyāṃsaṃ pāṃsuṃ tava caraṇapaṅkēruhabhavaṃ
viriñciḥ sañcinvanviracayati lōkānavikalam
vahatyēnaṃ śauriḥ kathamapi sahasrēṇa śirasāṃ
haraḥ saṅkṣudyainaṃ bhajati bhasitōddhūlanavidhim

The dust of Devi's feet grants Brahma creative power, Vishnu sustaining power, Shiva dissolving power.
