kṣitau ṣaṭpañcāśaddvisamadhikapañcāśadudakē
hutāśē dvāṣaṣṭiścaturadhikapañcāśadanilē
divi dviḥṣaṭtriṃśanmanasi ca catuḥṣaṣṭiriti yē
mayūkhāstēṣāmapyupari tava pādāmbujayugam

Devi is Sāvitrī — she shines with a radiance that surpasses the sun.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 14
