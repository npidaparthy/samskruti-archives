mahīṃ mūlādhārē kamapi maṇipūrē hutavahaṃ
sthitaṃ svādhiṣṭhānē hṛdi marutamākāśamupari
manō'pi bhrūmadhyē sakalamapi bhittvā kulapathaṃ
sahasrārē padmē saha rahasi patyā viharasē

All acts — japa, meditation, homa, eating, sleeping — become Devi's worship; her grace flows naturally.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 9
