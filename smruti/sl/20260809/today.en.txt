vahatyamba stambēramadanujakumbhaprakṛtibhiḥ
samārabdhāṃ muktāmaṇibhiramalāṃ hāralatikām
kucābhōgō bimbādhararucibhirantaḥ śabalitāṃ
pratāpavyāmiśrāṃ puradamayituḥ kīrtimiva tē

Devi's dance — in her dance the entire universe dissolves and is reborn with each step.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 74
