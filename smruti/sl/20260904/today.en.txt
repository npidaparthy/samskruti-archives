vipañcyā gāyantī vividhamapadānaṃ paśupatēḥ
tvayārabdhē vaktuṃ calitaśirasā sādhuvacanē
tadīyairmādhuryairapalapitatantrīkalaravāṃ
nijāṃ vīṇāṃ vāṇī niculayati cōlēna nibhṛtam

Devi's gentle smile is nectar; those who behold it find liberation very near.
