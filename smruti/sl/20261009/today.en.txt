kadā kālē mātaḥ kathaya kalitālaktakarasaṃ
pibēyaṃ vidyārthī tava caraṇanirṇējanajalam
prakṛtyā mūkānāmapi ca kavitākāraṇatayā
kadā dhattē vāṇīmukhakamalatāmbūlarasatām

The poet again wonders whether anyone can truly praise Devi — yet this loving attempt is itself her joy.
