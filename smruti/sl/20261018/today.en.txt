tvadīyaṃ saundaryaṃ tuhinagirikanyē tulayituṃ
kavīndrāḥ kalpantē kathamapi viriñciprabhṛtayaḥ
yadālōkautsukyādamaralalanā yānti manasā
tapōbhirduṣprāpāmapi giriśasāyujyapadavīm

Devi's eyes created the three worlds; her glance alone gives life to all existence.
