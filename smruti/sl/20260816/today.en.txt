purārātērantaḥpuramasi tatastvaccaraṇayōḥ
saparyāmaryādā taralakaraṇānāmasulabhā
tathā hyētē nītāḥ śatamakhamukhāḥ siddhimatulāṃ
tava dvārōpāntasthitibhiraṇimādyābhiramarāḥ

Shiva himself was the first devotee of Śrīvidyā; its mystery is the most profound secret.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 95
