dṛśā drāghīyasyā daradalitanīlōtpalarucā
davīyāṃsaṃ dīnaṃ snapaya kṛpayā māmapi śivē
anēnāyaṃ dhanyō bhavati na ca tē hāniriyatā
vanē vā harmyē vā samakaranipātō himakaraḥ

Devi's abdomen is slender and delicate — the maternal center that nourishes all the worlds.
