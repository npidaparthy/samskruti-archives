śrutīnāṃ mūrdhānō dadhati tava yau śēkharatayā
mamāpyētau mātaḥ śirasi dayayā dhēhi caraṇau
yayōḥ pādyaṃ pāthaḥ paśupatijaṭājūṭataṭinī
yayōrlākṣālakṣmīraruṇaharicūḍāmaṇiruciḥ

Devi in her eight-armed form bestows the eight siddhis; her eight hands are eight sovereignties.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 84
