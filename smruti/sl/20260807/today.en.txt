śivaḥ śaktyā yuktō yadi bhavati śaktaḥ prabhavituṃ
na cēdēvaṃ dēvō na khalu kuśalaḥ spanditumapi
atastvāmārādhyāṃ hariharaviriñcādibhirapi
praṇantuṃ stōtuṃ vā kathamakṛtapuṇyaḥ prabhavati

Shiva can act only when united with Shakti; without her he cannot stir. Salutation to Devi's feet.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 1
