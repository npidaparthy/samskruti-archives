kirīṭaṃ vairiñcaṃ parihara puraḥ kaiṭabhabhidaḥ
kaṭhōrē kōṭīrē skhalasi jahi jambhārimakuṭam
praṇamrēṣvētēṣu prasabhamupayātasya bhavanaṃ
bhavasyābhyutthānē tava parijanōktirvijayatē

Devi dwells in the nine enclosures of Śrī Chakra and administers all worlds from the central bindu.
