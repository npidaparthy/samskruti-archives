prakṛtyāraktāyāstava sudati dantacchadarucēḥ
pravakṣyē sādṛśyaṃ janayatu phalaṃ vidrumalatā
na bimbaṃ tadbimbapratiphalanarāgādaruṇitaṃ
tulāmadhyārōḍhuṃ kathamiva vilajjēta kalayā

Devi's lotus feet are tender and crimson; at her feet all sins and sorrows dissolve instantly.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 62
