tavāparṇē karṇējapanayanapaiśunyacakitāḥ
nilīyantē tōyē niyatamanimēṣāḥ śapharikāḥ
iyaṃ ca śrīrbaddhacchadapuṭakavāṭaṃ kuvalayaṃ
jahāti pratyūṣē niśi ca vighaṭayya praviśati

Devi's navel is the great lotus from which Brahma arose — the center of all creation.
