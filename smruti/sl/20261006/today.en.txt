smaraṃ yōniṃ lakṣmīṃ tritayamidamādau tava manōḥ
nidhāyaikē nityē niravadhimahābhōgarasikāḥ
bhajanti tvāṃ cintāmaṇiguṇanibaddhākṣavalayāḥ
śivāgnau juhvantaḥ surabhighṛtadhārāhutiśataiḥ

Devi is the transcendent supreme reality; experience of her is beyond all expression.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 33
