viśuddhau tē śuddhasphaṭikaviśadaṃ vyōmajanakaṃ
śivaṃ sēvē dēvīmapi śivasamānavyavasitām
yayōḥ kāntyā yāntyāḥ śaśikiraṇasārūpyasaraṇēḥ
vidhūtāntardhvāntā vilasati cakōrīva jagatī

Devi plays with the entire universe at her fingertip — such is her supreme power.
