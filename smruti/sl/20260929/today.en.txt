haristvāmārādhya praṇatajanasaubhāgyajananīṃ
purā nārī bhūtvā puraripumapi kṣōbhamanayat
smarōpi tvāṃ natvā ratinayanalēhyēna vapuṣā
munīnāmapyantaḥ prabhavati hi mōhāya mahatām

Brahma was born from Devi's navel lotus; the sun and moon emerged from her eyes.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 5
