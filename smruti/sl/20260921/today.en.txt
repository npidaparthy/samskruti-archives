bhruvau bhugnē kiñcidbhuvanabhayabhaṅgavyasanini
tvadīyē nētrābhyāṃ madhukararucibhyāṃ dhṛtaguṇam
dhanurmanyē savyētarakaragṛhītaṃ ratipatēḥ
prakōṣṭhē muṣṭau ca sthagayati nigūḍhāntaramumē

Devi's cheeks shine like golden lotus petals — words are inadequate for such beauty.
