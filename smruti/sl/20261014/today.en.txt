karīndrāṇāṃ śuṇḍānkanakakadalīkāṇḍapaṭalīṃ
ubhābhyāmūrubhyāmubhayamapi nirjitya bhavati
suvṛttābhyāṃ patyuḥ praṇatikaṭhinābhyāṃ girisutē
vidhijñē jānubhyāṃ vibudhakarikumbhadvayamasi

Devi is Ānanda-rūpiṇī — her very existence is bliss; her bliss is pure rapture.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 82
