smitajyōtsnājālaṃ tava vadanacandrasya pibatāṃ
cakōrāṇāmāsīdatirasatayā cañcujaḍimā
atastē śītāṃśōramṛtalaharīmāmlarucayaḥ
pibanti svacchandaṃ niśiniśi bhṛśaṃ kāñjikadhiyā

Devi's toenails gleam like diamonds, dispelling the darkness of ignorance in those seeking liberation.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 63
