pavitrīkartuṃ naḥ paśupatiparādhīnahṛdayē
dayāmitrairnētrairaruṇadhavalaśyāmarucibhiḥ
nadaḥ śōṇō gaṅgā tapanatanayēti dhruvamamuṃ
trayāṇāṃ tīrthānāmupanayasi sambhēdamanagham

From Devi's palm blessings pour on all worlds; her hand gives both fearlessness and boons.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 54
