naraṃ varṣīyāṃsaṃ nayanavirasaṃ narmasu jaḍaṃ
tavāpāṅgālōkē patitamanudhāvanti śataśaḥ
galadvēṇībandhāḥ kucakalaśavisrastasicayāḥ
haṭhāttruṭyatkāñcyō vigalitadukūlā yuvatayaḥ

Devi dwells in the Śrī Chakra — the geometric embodiment of her cosmic form.
