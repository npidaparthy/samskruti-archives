aviśrāntaṃ patyurguṇagaṇakathāmrēḍanajapā
japāpuṣpacchāyā tava janani jihvā jayati sā
yadagrāsīnāyāḥ sphaṭikadṛṣadacchacchavimayī
sarasvatyā mūrtiḥ pariṇamati māṇikyavapuṣā

Devi's feet are adorned with jeweled anklets — their tinkling sound sanctifies the universe.
