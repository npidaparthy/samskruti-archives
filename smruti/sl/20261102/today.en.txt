namōvākaṃ brūmō nayanaramaṇīyāya padayōḥ
tavāsmai dvandvāya sphuṭarucirasālaktakavatē
asūyatyatyantaṃ yadabhihananāya spṛhayatē
paśūnāmīśānaḥ pramadavanakaṅkēlitaravē

Devi is worshipped through the nine-enclosure (navāvaraṇa) pūjā; each enclosure houses a Mahāśakti.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 85
