kavīndrāṇāṃ cētaḥkamalavanabālātaparuciṃ
bhajantē yē santaḥ katicidaruṇāmēva bhavatīm
viriñciprēyasyāstaruṇataraśṛṅgāralaharī-
gabhīrābhirvāgbhirvidadhati satāṃ rañjanamamī

In the womb and after birth — in every state of life, Devi is the eternal mother.
