śarajjyōtsnāśuddhāṃ śaśiyutajaṭājūṭamakuṭāṃ
varatrāsatrāṇasphaṭikaghaṭikāpustakakarām
sakṛnna tvā natvā kathamiva satāṃ sannidadhatē
madhukṣīradrākṣāmadhurimadhurīṇāḥ phaṇitayaḥ

Devi is Gāyatrī — the primordial source from which all Vedic mantras arose.
