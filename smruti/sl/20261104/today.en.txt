śivaḥ śaktiḥ kāmaḥ kṣitiratha raviḥ śītakiraṇaḥ
smarō haṃsaḥ śakrastadanu ca parāmāraharayaḥ
amī hṛllēkhābhistisṛbhiravasānēṣu ghaṭitāḥ
bhajantē varṇāstē tava janani nāmāvayavatām

Devi's supreme throne is the heart of Shiva — she rules all worlds seated upon him.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 32
