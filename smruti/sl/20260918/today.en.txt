ahaḥ sūtē savyaṃ tava nayanamarkātmakatayā
triyāmāṃ vāmaṃ tē sṛjati rajanīnāyakatayā
tṛtīyā tē dṛṣṭirdaradalitahēmāmbujaruciḥ
samādhattē sandhyāṃ divasaniśayōrantaracarīm

Devi's nose is adorned with pearl-drops and graceful as a bee — a delicate fragrant beauty.

— సౌన్దర్యలహరీ · శ్లో. 48
