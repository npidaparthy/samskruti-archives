āpūryamāṇamacalapratiṣṭhaṃ
samudramāpaḥ praviśanti yadvat
tadvatkāmāyaṃ praviśanti sarve
sa śāntimāpnoti na kāmakāmī

As the ocean remains full despite the waters of many rivers flowing into it — the person into whom all desires enter but who remains unmoved, attains peace; not so the one who desires.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 2.70
