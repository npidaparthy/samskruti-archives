arjuna uvāca
prakṛtiṃ puruṣaṃ caiva
kṣetraṃ kṣetrajñam eva ca
etad veditum icchāmi

Arjuna said — O Keśava, I wish to know about prakṛti (matter) and puruṣa (consciousness), the field and the knower of the field, knowledge and what is to be known.
