jñeyaḥ sa nityasannyāsī
yo na dveṣṭi na kāṅkṣati
nirdvandvo hi mahābāho
sukhaṃ bandhātpramucyate

One who neither hates nor desires (is free from dualities) is to be known as a constant renunciant. Free from duality, that person is easily freed from bondage, O mighty-armed.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 5.3
