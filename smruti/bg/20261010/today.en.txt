idaṃ tu te guhyatamaṃ
pravakṣyāmyanasūyave
jñānaṃ vijñānasahitaṃ
yajjñātvā mokṣyase'śubhāt

The Lord says — I shall teach you this most secret teaching, for you are without envy (toward Me). Knowing this, you shall be freed from inauspiciousness.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 9.1
