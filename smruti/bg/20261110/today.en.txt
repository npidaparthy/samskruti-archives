abhyāse'pyasamartho'si
matkarmaparamo bhava
madarthamapi karmāṇi
kurvansiddhimavāpsyasi

If you are unable to do even this abhyāsa, then be devoted to performing action for My sake — performing actions for My sake, you shall attain perfection.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 12.10
