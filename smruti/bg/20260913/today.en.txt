dūreṇa hyavaraṃ karma
buddhiyogāddhanañjaya
buddhau śaraṇamanviccha
kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ

Far inferior indeed is action (done from desire) compared to the yoga of wisdom, O Dhanañjaya. Seek refuge in wisdom. Pitiable are those who are motivated by fruit.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 2.49
