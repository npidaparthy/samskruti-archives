yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya
vartate kāmakārataḥ
na sa siddhimavāpnoti
na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim

One who, having abandoned the injunctions of scripture, acts under the influence of desire — does not attain perfection, happiness, or the Supreme Goal.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 16.23
