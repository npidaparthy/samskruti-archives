suhṛnmitrāryudāsīna-
madhyasthadveṣyabandhuṣu
sādhuṣvapi ca pāpeṣu
samabuddhirviśiṣyate

One who is equal toward well-wishers, friends, enemies, the neutral, the mediators, the hateful, the relatives, the virtuous, and the sinful — that person excels.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 6.9
