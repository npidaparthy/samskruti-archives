tadbuddhayastadātmānaḥ
tanniṣṭhāstatparāyaṇāḥ
gacchantyapunarāvṛttiṃ
jñānanirdhūtakalmaṣāḥ

Those whose intelligence is absorbed in That, whose self is fixed in That, who have That as the highest, who are devoted to That — these go, not returning, their impurities destroyed by knowledge.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 5.17
