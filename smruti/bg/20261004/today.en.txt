sahayajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā
purovāca prajāpatiḥ
anena prasaviṣyadhvam
eṣa vo'stviṣṭakāmadhuk

In the beginning, having created beings along with sacrifice, the Creator said: By this you shall multiply; let this (yajña) be your wish-fulfilling cow.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 3.10
