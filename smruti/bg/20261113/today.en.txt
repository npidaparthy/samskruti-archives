jñānaṃ karma ca kartā ca
tridhaiva guṇabhedataḥ
procyate guṇasaṅkhyāne
yathāvacchṛṇu tānyapi

Knowledge, action, and the agent — these are declared in Sāṃkhya to be of three kinds, depending on the difference of guṇas. Hear these also completely.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 18.19
