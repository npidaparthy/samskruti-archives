tvamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ veditavyaṃ
tvamasya viśvasya paraṃ nidhānam
tvamavyayaḥ śāśvatadharmagoptā
sanātanastvaṃ puruṣo mato me

You are the Imperishable, the supreme to be known. You are the supreme refuge of this universe. You are the undying guardian of eternal dharma. You are the primeval Person — I believe this.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 11.18
