prajahāti yadā kāmān
sarvānpārtha manogatān
ātmanyevātmanā tuṣṭaḥ
sthitaprajñastadocyate

The Blessed Lord said: O Arjuna, when a person completely abandons all the desires of the mind and is satisfied in the Self by the Self — that person is called a sthitaprajña (one of steady wisdom).

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 2.55
