tasmātpraṇamya praṇidhāya kāyaṃ
prasādaye tvāmahamīśamīḍyam
piteva putrasya sakheva sakhyuḥ
priyaḥ priyāyārhasi deva soḍhum

Therefore, bowing down and prostrating my body, I ask Your grace, O Lord, worthy of praise. As a father to a son, as a friend to a friend, as a lover to a beloved — please bear with me, O God.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 11.44
