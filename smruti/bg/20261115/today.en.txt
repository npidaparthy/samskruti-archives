ahiṃsā satyamakrodhaḥ
tyāgaḥ śāntirapaiśunam
dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ
mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam

Non-harming, truth, freedom from anger, renunciation, tranquility, non-slandering, compassion for beings, freedom from covetousness, gentleness, modesty, absence of fickleness —
