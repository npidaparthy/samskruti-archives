yajñaśiṣṭāmṛtabhujaḥ
yānti brahma sanātanam
nāyaṃ loko'styayajñasya
kuto'nyaḥ kurusattama

Those who eat the nectar of sacrifice left over after the sacrifice go to the eternal Brahman. This world is not for one who does not sacrifice; how then the next world, O best of Kurus?

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 4.31
