na māṃ duṣkṛtino mūḍhāḥ
prapadyante narādhamāḥ
māyayāpahṛtajñānāḥ
āsuraṃ bhāvamāśritāḥ

The evildoers, the deluded, the lowest of humans, those whose knowledge is destroyed by māyā, and those with a demonic nature — these four do not take refuge in Me.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 7.15
