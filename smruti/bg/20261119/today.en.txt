yatra yogeśvaraḥ kṛṣṇaḥ
yatra pārtho dhanurdharaḥ
tatra śrīrvijayo bhūtiḥ
dhruvā nītirmatirmama

Wherever there is Krishna, the Lord of Yoga, and wherever there is Pārtha, the archer — there will be prosperity, victory, abundance, and unfaltering right conduct. This is my view.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 18.78
