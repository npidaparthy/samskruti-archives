mānāpamānayostulyaḥ
tulyo mitrāripakṣayoḥ
sarvārambhaparityāgī
guṇātītaḥ sa ucyate

Who is the same in honor and dishonor, the same toward friend's and enemy's faction — one who has renounced all undertakings — is said to have transcended the guṇas.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 14.25
