na rūpamasyeha tathopalabhyate
nānto na cādirna ca sampratiṣṭhā
aśvatthamenaṃ suvirūḍhamūlam
asaṅgaśastreṇa dṛḍhena chittvā

The real form of this tree cannot be perceived here — its beginning, end, or foundation cannot be found — cut down this deep-rooted tree with the weapon of non-attachment.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 15.3
