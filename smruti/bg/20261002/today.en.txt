indriyāṇi parāṇyāhuḥ
indriyebhyaḥ paraṃ manaḥ
manasastu parā buddhiḥ
yo buddheḥ paratastu saḥ

They say the senses are superior to the sense-objects; the mind is superior to the senses; the intellect is superior to the mind; but higher than the intellect is the Self.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 3.42
