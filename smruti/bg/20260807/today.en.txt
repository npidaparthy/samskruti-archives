brahmārpaṇaṃ brahma haviḥ
brahmāgnau brahmaṇā hutam
brahmaiva tena gantavyaṃ
brahmakarmasamādhinā

Brahman is the offering; Brahman is the oblation poured by Brahman into the fire of Brahman. Brahman alone is to be reached by one who is absorbed in the action of Brahman.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 4.24
