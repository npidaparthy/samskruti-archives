śreyānsvadharmo viguṇaḥ
paradharmātsvanuṣṭhitāt
svadharme nidhanaṃ śreyaḥ
paradharmo bhayāvahaḥ

Better is one's own duty, though imperfectly performed, than the duty of another even if well performed. Death in performing one's own duty is preferable; the duty of another is fraught with danger.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 3.35
