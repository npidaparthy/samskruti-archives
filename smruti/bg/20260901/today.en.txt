samaduḥkhasukhaḥ svasthaḥ
samaloṣṭāśmakāñcanaḥ
tulyapriyāpriyo dhīraḥ
tulyanindātmasaṃstutiḥ

Who is the same in pleasure and pain, self-abiding, for whom a clod of earth, stone, and gold are the same; who is the same to the pleasant and unpleasant —

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 14.24
