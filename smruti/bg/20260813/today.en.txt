bāhyasparśeṣvasaktātmā
vindatyātmani yatsukham
sa brahmayogayuktātmā
sukhamakṣayamaśnute

One who has a self not attached to external contact, finding happiness in the Self — that one, having a self yoked to the yoga of Brahman, attains imperishable happiness.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 5.21
