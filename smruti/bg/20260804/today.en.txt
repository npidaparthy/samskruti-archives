bandhurātmātmanastasya
yenātmaivātmanā jitaḥ
anātmanastu śatrutve
vartetātmaiva śatruvat

The self is the friend of the self for one who has conquered the self by the self. For one who has not conquered the self, the self acts as an enemy, like an external foe.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 6.6
