santuṣṭaḥ satataṃ yogī
yatātmā dṛḍhaniścayaḥ
mayyarpitamanobuddhiḥ
yo madbhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ

— ever content, the yogī whose self is controlled, whose conviction is firm, whose mind and intellect are surrendered to Me — that devotee of Mine is dear to Me.

— శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా 12.14
