sarvaśāstrārthatattvajñassmṛtimānpratibhānavān
sarvalōkapriyassādhuradīnātmā vicakṣaṇaḥ

A knower of the essential meaning of all the śāstras, of good memory, gifted with insight; beloved of all the world, virtuous, undaunted in spirit, and discerning.
