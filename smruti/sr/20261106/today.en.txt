karmaṇā tēna mahatā trailōkyaṃ sacarācaram
sadēvarṣigaṇaṃ tuṣṭaṃ rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ

By that great deed of the great-souled Rāghava, the three worlds — with all moving and unmoving beings, and the multitude of gods and sages — were satisfied.
