nandigrāmē jaṭāṃ hitvā bhrātṛbhissahitōnaghaḥ
rāmassītāmanuprāpya rājyaṃ punaravāptavān

At Nandigrāma, having shed his matted locks, the sinless Rāma, together with his brothers, having regained Sītā, obtained the kingdom once more.
