samassamavibhaktāṅgassnigdhavarṇaḥ pratāpavān
pīnavakṣā viśālākṣō lakṣmīvān śubhalakṣaṇaḥ

Symmetrical, with well-proportioned limbs, of lustrous complexion, majestic; broad-chested, large-eyed, fortunate, and marked with auspicious signs.
