tapassvādhyāyanirataṃ tapasvī vāgvidāṃ varam
nāradaṃ paripapraccha vālmīkirmunipuṅgavam

The sage Vālmīki, foremost among munis, asked Nārada — who was devoted to austerity and Vedic study, and was the best of the eloquent — this question.
