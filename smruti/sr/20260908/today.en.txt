sōdhigamya mahātmānaṃ kṛtvā rāmaṃ pradakṣiṇam
nyavēdayadamēyātmā dṛṣṭā sītēti tattvataḥ

He, having approached the great-souled one, having circumambulated Rāma, the immeasurable-souled [Hanumān] reported: 'Sītā has been seen' — truly.

— శ్రీమద్రామాయణకథాసంక్షేపః · శ్లో. 78
