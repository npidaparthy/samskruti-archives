prahṛṣṭamuditō lōkastuṣṭaḥ puṣṭassudhārmikaḥ
nirāmayō hyarōgaśca durbhikṣabhayavarjitaḥ

The people, delighted and rejoicing, were content, prosperous, and highly righteous — free from disease, healthy, and without fear of famine.
