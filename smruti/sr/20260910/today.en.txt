idaṃ pavitraṃ pāpaghnaṃ puṇyaṃ vēdaiśca sammitam
yaḥ paṭhēdrāmacaritaṃ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyatē

This account is pure, destroys sin, meritorious, and equal in worth to the Vedas. Whoever recites this life-story of Rāma is freed from all sins.
