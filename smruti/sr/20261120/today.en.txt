bhrātaraṃ dayitō bhrātussaubhrātramanudarśayan
rāmasya dayitā bhāryā nityaṃ prāṇasamā hitā

The beloved brother, showing true brotherly devotion toward his brother; and Rāma's beloved wife, ever dear as his own life-breath and devoted to him.

— శ్రీమద్రామాయణకథాసంక్షేపః · శ్లో. 26
