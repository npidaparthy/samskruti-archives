mahōraskō mahēṣvāsō gūḍhajatrurarindamaḥ
ājānubāhussuśirāssulalāṭassuvikramaḥ

Broad-chested, a mighty archer, with well-formed collarbones, a subduer of foes — arms reaching his knees, a fine head, a handsome forehead, and excellent gait.
