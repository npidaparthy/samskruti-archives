dēvagandharvasaṅkāśāstatra tē nyavasan sukham
citrakūṭaṃ gatē rāmē putraśōkāturastathā

Resembling gods and gandharvas, they dwelt there happily. And when Rāma had gone to Citrakūṭa, [Daśaratha,] afflicted with grief for his son...
