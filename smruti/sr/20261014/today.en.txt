astrēṇōnmuktamātmānaṃ jñātvā paitāmahādvarāt
marṣayanrākṣasānvīrō yantriṇastānyadṛcchayā

The hero, knowing himself freed from the [Brahmā's] weapon by the grandsire's boon, out of his own will tolerated the rākṣasas who bound him.
