dēvatābhyō varaṃ prāpya samutthāpya ca vānarān
ayōdhyāṃ prasthitō rāmaḥ puṣpakēṇa suhṛdvṛtaḥ

Having received a boon from the gods, and having revived the fallen monkeys, Rāma set out for Ayōdhyā in the Puṣpaka [chariot], surrounded by friends.
