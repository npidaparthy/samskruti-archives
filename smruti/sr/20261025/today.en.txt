paṭhandvijō vāgṛṣabhatvamīyāt syātkṣatriyō bhūmipatitvamīyāt
vaṇigjanaḥ paṇyaphalatvamīyāt janaśca śūdrōpi mahatvamīyāt

A brāhmaṇa reciting it attains eminence in speech; a kṣatriya would attain sovereignty over the earth; a merchant attains profit in trade; and even a person of the śūdra class attains greatness.
