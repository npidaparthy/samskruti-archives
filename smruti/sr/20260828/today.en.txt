nivēdayitvābhijñānaṃ pravṛttiṃ ca nivēdya ca
samāśvāsya ca vaidēhīṃ mardayāmāsa tōraṇam

Having presented the token [of recognition], and having related the news, and having comforted Vaidēhī, he then destroyed the gateway [in a rampage].
