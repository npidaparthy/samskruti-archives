śṛṅgibērapurē sūtaṃ gaṅgākūlē vyasarjayat
guhamāsādya dharmātmā niṣādādhipatiṃ priyam

At Śṛṅgiberapura, on the bank of the Gaṅgā, the righteous-souled one, having met his dear friend Guha, the lord of the Niṣādas, sent back his charioteer.
