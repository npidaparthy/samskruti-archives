rakṣitā svasya dharmasya svajanasya ca rakṣitā
vēdavēdāṅgatattvajñō dhanurvēdē ca niṣṭhitaḥ

Protector of his own dharma, and protector of his own people; a knower of the essence of the Vedas and Vedāṅgas, accomplished in the science of archery.
