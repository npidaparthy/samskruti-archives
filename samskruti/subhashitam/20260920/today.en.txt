ahiṃsā paramo dharmaḥ tathāhiṃsā paro damaḥ ।
ahiṃsā paramaṃ dānaṃ ahiṃsā paramaṃ tapaḥ ॥

Non-violence is the highest dharma; non-violence is the greatest self-restraint; non-violence is the supreme charity; non-violence is the ultimate austerity.

— Mahābhārata — Anuśāsana Parva 115.23
