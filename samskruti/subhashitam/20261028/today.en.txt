kāmaṃ krodhaṃ lobhaṃ mohaṃ
tyaktvātmānaṃ bhāvaya ko'ham ।
ātmajñānavihīnā mūḍhāḥ
te pacyante narakanigūḍhāḥ ॥

Give up desire, anger, greed, and delusion, and inquire, 'Who am I?' Fools devoid of self-knowledge are consumed, sunk deep in torment.

— Bhaja Govindam (Adi Shankaracharya)
