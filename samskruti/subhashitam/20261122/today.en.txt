vayasi gate kaḥ kāmavikāraḥ śuṣke nīre kaḥ kāsāraḥ ।
kṣīṇe vitte kaḥ parivāro jñāte tattve kaḥ saṃsāraḥ ॥

When youth passes, where is passion's disturbance? When water dries, where is the lake? When wealth runs out, where are the relatives? When truth is known, where is the cycle of existence?

— Ādi Śaṅkarācārya, Bhaja Govindam, verse 13
