aniṣṭamiṣṭaṃ miśraṃ ca trividhaṃ karmaṇaḥ phalam ।
bhavatyatyāgināṃ pretya na tu sannyāsināṃ kvacit ॥

The threefold fruit of action — undesirable, desirable, and mixed — comes after death to those who have not renounced attachment to it. For the truly renounced, it never comes at all.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
