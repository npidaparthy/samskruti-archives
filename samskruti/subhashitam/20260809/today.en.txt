gururbrahmā gururviṣṇuḥ gururdevo maheśvaraḥ ।
guruḥ sākṣāt parabrahma tasmai śrī gurave namaḥ ॥

The guru is Brahma (creator), the guru is Viṣṇu (sustainer), the guru is Maheśvara (Śiva, the transformer). The guru is directly the Supreme Brahman. Salutations to that revered guru.

— Guru Stotram (Skānda Purāṇa)
