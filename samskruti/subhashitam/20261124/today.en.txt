dhanadhānyaprayogeṣu vidyāsaṅgrahaṇeṣu ca ।
āhāre vyavahāre ca tyaktalajjaḥ sukhī bhavet ॥

One who casts off false shame or hesitation in the use of wealth and grain, in the pursuit of knowledge, in eating, and in daily dealings — such a person becomes happy.

— Chanakya Niti
