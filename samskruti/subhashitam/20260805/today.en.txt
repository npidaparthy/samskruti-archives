dinamapi rajanī sāyaṃ prātaḥ śiśiravasantau punarāyātaḥ ।
kālaḥ krīḍati gacchatyāyuḥ tadapi na muñcatyāśāvāyuḥ ॥

Day and night, evening and morning, winter and spring return again and again. Time plays on, life passes away — yet the wind of desire does not release its hold.

— Bhaja Govindam — Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (verse 20)
