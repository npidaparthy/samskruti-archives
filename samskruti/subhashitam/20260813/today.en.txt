dhyāyato viṣayānpuṃsaḥ saṅgasteṣūpajāyate ।
saṅgātsañjāyate kāmaḥ kāmātkrodho'bhijāyate ॥

In a man who keeps dwelling in thought on sense-objects, attachment to them arises; from attachment is born desire; and from desire is born anger.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
