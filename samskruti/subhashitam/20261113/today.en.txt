sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata ।
śraddhāmayo'yaṃ puruṣo yo yacchraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ ॥

O Bharata! Everyone's faith accords with their own inner nature. This being is made of faith — whatever a person's faith is, that alone is what they truly are.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Śraddhātraya Vibhāga Yoga)
