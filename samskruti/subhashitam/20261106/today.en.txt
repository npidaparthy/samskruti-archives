kā te kāntā kaste putraḥ saṃsāro'yamatīva vicitraḥ ।
kasya tvaṃ kaḥ kuta āyātaḥ tattvaṃ cintaya tadiha bhrātaḥ ॥

Who is your wife? Who is your son? This saṃsāra is exceedingly wondrous. Whose are you? Who are you? From where have you come? O brother! Contemplate that truth right here, right now.

— Bhaja Govindam — Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (verse 14)
