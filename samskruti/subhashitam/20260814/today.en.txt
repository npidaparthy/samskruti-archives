duḥkheṣvanudvignamanāḥ sukheṣu vigataspṛhaḥ ।
vītarāgabhayakrodhaḥ sthitadhīrmunirucyate ॥

One whose mind is not disturbed amid sorrows, who is free of craving amid pleasures, and who has cast off passion, fear, and anger — such a one is called a sage of steady wisdom.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
