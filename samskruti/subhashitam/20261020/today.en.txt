saṅgaṃ tyaktvā phalaṃ caiva kartā triguṇasaṃśrayaḥ ।
bhaktyā bhāvaṃ viśuddhātmā vikarmaṇyapi karmasu ॥

One who acts having given up both attachment and desire for the fruit is of purified mind. Whatever action he performs, he does not fall under the sway of the three gunas.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
