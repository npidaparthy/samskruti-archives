vane ramye vasedyogī vāriṇyagnau ca nirmale ।
vāyumātreṇa santuṣṭaḥ na sukhaṃ vindate yathā ॥

A yogi may dwell in a lovely forest, content with pure water and fire, satisfied even with air alone — yet if the mind itself is not at peace, happiness will still elude him.

— Subhashita Ratnabhandagara
