bhaja govindaṃ bhaja govindaṃ govindaṃ bhaja mūḍhamate ।
samprāpte sannihite kāle nahi nahi rakṣati ḍukṛñ karaṇe ॥

O fool! Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, worship Govinda. When the final hour arrives, grammatical rules (worldly learning without devotion) will certainly not save you.

— Bhaja Govindam — Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (verse 1)
