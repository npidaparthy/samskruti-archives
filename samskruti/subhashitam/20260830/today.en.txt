anabhyāse viṣaṃ vidyā ajīrṇe bhojanaṃ viṣam ।
daridrasya viṣaṃ sabhā vṛddhasya taruṇī viṣam ॥

Learning without practice is poison; food eaten while the previous meal remains undigested is poison; a royal court/gathering is poison for a poor man; a young wife is poison for an old man.

— Subhashita Ratnabhandagara
