yallabdhvā na kiñcidvāñchati na śocati na dveṣṭi na ramate ॥
notsāhī bhavati ॥

That devotion which, once attained, leaves a person desiring nothing further, grieving not, hating not, delighting not in the ordinary sense, nor growing excited in the worldly way — that is true devotion.

— Narada Bhakti Sutras
