yuktāhāravihārasya yuktaceṣṭasya karmasu ।
yuktasvapnāvabodhasya yogo bhavati duḥkhahā ॥

For one whose eating and recreation are regulated, whose actions are balanced, and whose sleep and waking are disciplined — yoga becomes the destroyer of sorrow.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Dhyāna Yoga)
