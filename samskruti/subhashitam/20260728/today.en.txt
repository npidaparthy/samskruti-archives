karmaṇyevādhikāraste mā phaleṣu kadācana ।
mā karmaphalaheturbhūrmā te saṅgo'stvakarmaṇi ॥

You have the right to perform your duty only, never to the fruits thereof. Let not the fruit of action be your motive, nor let there be attachment to inaction.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
