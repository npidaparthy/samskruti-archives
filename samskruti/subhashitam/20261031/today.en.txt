upādhyāyāndaśācārya ācāryāṇāṃ śataṃ pitā ।
sahasraṃ tu pitṝnmātā gauraveṇātiricyate ॥

One preceptor outweighs ten ordinary teachers in worth; a father outweighs a hundred such preceptors; and a mother surpasses a thousand fathers in the reverence she deserves.

— Manusmriti
