śanaiḥ śanairuparamedbuddhyā dhṛtigṛhītayā ।
ātmasaṃsthaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā na kiñcidapi cintayet ॥

With an intellect held steady by firm resolve, one should gradually become still. Having fixed the mind upon the Self, one should think of nothing else at all.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Dhyāna Yoga)
