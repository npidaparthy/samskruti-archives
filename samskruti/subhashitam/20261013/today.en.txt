śāntākāraṃ bhujagaśayanaṃ padmanābhaṃ sureśam
viśvādhāraṃ gaganasadṛśaṃ meghavarṇaṃ śubhāṅgam ।
lakṣmīkāntaṃ kamalanayanaṃ yogibhirdhyānagamyam
vande viṣṇuṃ bhavabhayaharaṃ sarvalokaikanātham ॥

I salute Viṣṇu — of peaceful form, reclining on the serpent, lotus-naveled, Lord of the gods, support of the universe, all-pervading like the sky, cloud-hued, of auspicious limbs, beloved of Lakṣmī, lotus-eyed, attainable by yogis through meditation, remover of the fear of worldly existence, and the sole Lord of all worlds.

— Viṣṇu Dhyāna Śloka (traditional Pañcāyatana worship)
