satyaṃ mātā pitā jñānaṃ dharmo bhrātā dayā sakhā ।
śāntiḥ patnī kṣamā putraḥ ṣaḍete mama bāndhavāḥ ॥

Truth is my mother, knowledge my father, dharma my brother, compassion my friend, peace my wife, and forgiveness my son — these six are my true kin.

— Subhashita Ratnabhandagara
