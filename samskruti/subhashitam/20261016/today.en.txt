āpūryamāṇamacalapratiṣṭhaṃ
samudramāpaḥ praviśanti yadvat ।
tadvatkāmā yaṃ praviśanti sarve
sa śāntimāpnoti na kāmakāmī ॥

As waters flow into the ocean which, though ever filling, remains unmoved, so too the one into whom all desires enter without disturbing him attains peace — not the one who chases after desires.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
