bhaktyā māmabhijānāti yāvānyaścāsmi tattvataḥ ।
tato māṃ tattvato jñātvā viśate tadanantaram ॥

Through devotion alone one comes to know Me in truth — what and who I am. Having known Me truly, one immediately thereafter enters into Me.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
