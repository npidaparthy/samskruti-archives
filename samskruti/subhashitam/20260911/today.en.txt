śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa ।
sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate ॥

O Parantapa! The sacrifice of knowledge is superior to the sacrifice performed with material objects. O Partha! All action, in its entirety, culminates in knowledge.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Jñāna Karma Sannyāsa Yoga)
