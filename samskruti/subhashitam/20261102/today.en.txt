śraddhāvān labhate jñānaṃ tatparaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ ।
jñānaṃ labdhvā parāṃ śāntimacireṇādhigacchati ॥

One who has faith, who is devoted to gaining knowledge, and who has mastered the senses — attains knowledge. Having attained knowledge, one swiftly reaches supreme peace.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Jñāna Karma Sannyāsa Yoga)
