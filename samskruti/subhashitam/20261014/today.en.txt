vāsāṃsi jīrṇāni yathā vihāya navāni gṛhṇāti naro'parāṇi ।
tathā śarīrāṇi vihāya jīrṇānyanyāni saṃyāti navāni dehī ॥

As a person puts on new garments giving up worn-out ones, the embodied soul similarly casts off worn-out bodies and takes on new ones.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
