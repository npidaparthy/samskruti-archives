caturvidhā bhajante māṃ janāḥ sukṛtino'rjuna ।
ārto jijñāsurarthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha ॥

O Arjuna! Four kinds of virtuous people worship me — the distressed, the seeker of knowledge, the seeker of wealth, and the man of knowledge, O best of the Bharatas!

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Jñāna Vijñāna Yoga)
