raso'hamapsu kaunteya prabhāsmi śaśisūryayoḥ ।
praṇavaḥ sarvavedeṣu śabdaḥ khe pauruṣaṃ nṛṣu ॥

O son of Kunti! I am the taste in water; I am the light in the moon and sun; I am the syllable Om in all the Vedas; I am sound in ether; I am manly vigour in men.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Jñāna Vijñāna Yoga)
