aṅgaṃ galitaṃ palitaṃ muṇḍaṃ
daśanavihīnaṃ jātaṃ tuṇḍam ।
vṛddho yāti gṛhītvā daṇḍaṃ
tadapi na muñcatyāśāpiṇḍam ॥

The body has withered, the head has greyed, the mouth is toothless; the old man totters along clutching his staff — yet even now he will not let go of his bundle of hopes.

— Bhaja Govindam (Adi Shankaracharya)
