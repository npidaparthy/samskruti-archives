satsaṅgatve nissaṅgatvaṃ
nissaṅgatve nirmohatvam ।
nirmohatve niścalatattvaṃ
niścalatattve jīvanmuktiḥ ॥

From good company comes non-attachment; from non-attachment, freedom from delusion; from freedom from delusion, unshakable knowledge of truth; and from that steadfast truth, liberation while still alive.

— Bhaja Govindam (Adi Shankaracharya)
