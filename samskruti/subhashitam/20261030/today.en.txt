adhiṣṭhānaṃ tathā kartā karaṇaṃ ca pṛthagvidham ।
vividhāśca pṛthakceṣṭā daivaṃ caivātra pañcamam ॥

The body, the doer, the various instruments (senses), the manifold efforts, and fate as the fifth — these are the five causes behind every action.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
