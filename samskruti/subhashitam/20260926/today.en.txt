sahajaṃ karma kaunteya sadoṣamapi na tyajet ।
sarvārambhāhi doṣeṇa dhūmenāgnirivāvṛtāḥ ॥

O son of Kuntī, one should not abandon one's innate duty, even if it is imperfect. As fire is covered by smoke, all undertakings are covered by imperfection.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
