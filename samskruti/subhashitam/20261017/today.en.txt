yajñaśiṣṭāśinaḥ santo mucyante sarvakilbiṣaiḥ ।
bhuñjate te tvaghaṃ pāpā ye pacantyātmakāraṇāt ॥

The good who eat what remains after an offering are freed from all sins. Those who cook only for their own sake — those sinners eat sin itself.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Karma Yoga)
