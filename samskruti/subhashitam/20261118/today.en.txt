sā tvasmin paramapremarūpā ॥
amṛtasvarūpā ca ॥
yallabdhvā pumān siddho bhavati
amṛto bhavati tṛpto bhavati ॥

Bhakti is of the nature of supreme love for Him; it is of the nature of nectar itself. Gaining it, a person becomes fulfilled, becomes immortal, becomes fully content.

— Narada Bhakti Sutras
