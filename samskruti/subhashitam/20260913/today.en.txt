tṛṣṇāṃ chindhi bhaja kṣamāṃ jahi madaṃ pāpe ratiṃ mā kṛthāḥ
satyaṃ brūhyanuyāhi sādhupadavīṃ sevasva vidvajjanam ।
mānyānmānaya vidviṣo'pyanunaya pracchādayātmaguṇān
kīrtiṃ pālaya duḥkhiteṣu kuru dāṃ citte dayāmātatām ॥

Cut off craving; embrace forgiveness; destroy pride; take no delight in wrongdoing; speak the truth; follow the path of the virtuous; serve the learned; honour those who deserve honour; conciliate even your enemies; keep your own virtues hidden; guard your good name; give to the suffering; and let compassion spread wide in your heart.

— Bhartrhari's Niti Shataka
