karmāyattaṃ phalaṃ puṃsāṃ buddhiḥ karmānusāriṇī ।
tathāpi sudhiyaścāryāḥ suvicāryaiva kurvate ॥

Men's results follow their past karma, and even the intellect moves along karma's grooves. Even so, the wise and the noble act only after careful deliberation.

— Panchatantra / subhashita tradition
