oṃ dyauḥ śāntirantarikṣaṃ śāntiḥ
pṛthivī śāntirāpaḥ śāntiroṣadhayaḥ śāntiḥ ।
vanaspatayaḥ śāntirviśvedevāḥ śāntirbrahma śāntiḥ
sarvaṃ śāntiḥ śāntireva śāntiḥ sā mā śāntiredhi ॥

May the heavens be peace, the atmosphere be peace, the earth be peace, the waters be peace, the herbs be peace, the trees be peace, all the gods be peace, Brahman be peace, everything be peace. Peace itself is peace. May that peace come to me.

— Yajurveda (Shanti Patha)
