sahayajñāḥ prajāḥ sṛṣṭvā purovāca prajāpatiḥ ।
anena prasaviṣyadhvameṣa vo'stviṣṭakāmadhuk ॥

The Creator, having brought forth beings together with sacrifice, declared of old: 'By this may you prosper; may this be your wish-fulfilling cow.'

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Karma Yoga)
