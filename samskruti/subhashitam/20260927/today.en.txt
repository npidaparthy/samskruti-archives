traiguṇyaviṣayā vedā nistraiguṇyo bhavārjuna ।
nirdvandvo nityasattvastho niryogakṣema ātmavān ॥

The Vedas deal with matters within the domain of the three gunas. O Arjuna, be beyond the three gunas, free of the pairs of opposites, ever established in truth, unconcerned with gain and preservation, and self-possessed.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
