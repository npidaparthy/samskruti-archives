ayaṃ nijaḥ paro veti gaṇanā laghucetasām ।
udāracaritānāṃ tu vasudhaiva kuṭumbakam ॥

The reckoning of 'this one is mine, that one is a stranger' belongs to the narrow-minded. For those of noble character, the entire earth is one family.

— Mahopaniṣat 6.71 (also cited in Pañcatantra)
