tyajedekaṃ kulasyārthe grāmasyārthe kulaṃ tyajet ।
grāmaṃ janapadasyārthe ātmārthe pṛthivīṃ tyajet ॥

For the sake of the family, one may abandon a single person; for the sake of the village, the family; for the sake of the country, the village; and for the sake of one's own soul and dharma, even the whole earth.

— Chanakya Niti
