santuṣṭo bhāryayā bhartā bhartrā bhāryā tathaiva ca ।
yasminneva kule nityaṃ kalyāṇaṃ tatra vai dhruvam ॥

A husband content with his wife, a wife equally content with her husband — in whichever family this holds true, there prosperity remains constant forever.

— Manusmriti
