yatkaroṣi yadaśnāsi yajjuhoṣi dadāsi yat ।
yattapasyasi kaunteya tatkuruṣva madarpaṇam ॥

O son of Kuntī, whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer, whatever you give, whatever austerity you practise — do all that as an offering to Me.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Rāja Vidyā Rāja Guhya Yoga)
