dharmo viśvasya jagataḥ pratiṣṭhā ।
loke dharmiṣṭhaṃ prajā upasarpanti ।
dharmeṇa pāpamapanudati ।
dharme sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam ।
tasmāddharmaṃ paramaṃ vadanti ॥

Dharma is the foundation of the entire universe. People in this world draw near the one most established in dharma. By dharma evil is driven away. On dharma everything rests. Therefore they declare dharma supreme.

— Mahanarayana Upanishad (Taittiriya Aranyaka)
