mātarmedini tāta māruta sakhe tejaḥ subandho jala
bhrātarvyoma nibaddha eṣa bhavatāmantyaḥ praṇāmāñjaliḥ ।
yuṣmatsaṅgavaśopajātasukṛtasphārasphurannirmala-
jñānāpāstasamastamohamahimā līye pare brahmaṇi ॥

Mother Earth, Father Wind, Friend Fire, Kinsman Water, Brother Sky — to you all, this final salutation with folded hands. Through the merit of your long companionship, stainless knowledge has dawned and swept away all delusion — I now dissolve into the supreme Brahman.

— Bhartrhari's Vairagya Shataka
