tryambakaṃ yajāmahe sugandhiṃ puṣṭivardhanam ।
urvārukamiva bandhanānmṛtyormukṣīya māmṛtāt ॥

We worship the fragrant, all-nourishing three-eyed Lord. As a ripe cucumber falls free from its vine of its own accord, may I be released from the bondage of death — but not from immortality.

— Rigveda (Mahamrityunjaya Mantra)
