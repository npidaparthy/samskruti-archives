yathā dīpo nivātastho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā ।
yogino yatacittasya yuñjato yogamātmanaḥ ॥

As a lamp in a windless place does not flicker, so is the simile remembered for the yogi of controlled mind, practising the discipline of the Self.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Dhyāna Yoga)
