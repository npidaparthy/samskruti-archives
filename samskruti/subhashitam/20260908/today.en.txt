karacaraṇakṛtaṃ vākkāyajaṃ karmajaṃ vā
śravaṇanayanajaṃ vā mānasaṃ vāparādham ।
vihitamavihitaṃ vā sarvametatkṣamasva
jaya jaya karuṇābdhe śrīmahādeva śambho ॥

Whatever offence committed by hand or foot, by speech or body, by action, by ear or eye, or by the mind — whether done knowingly or unknowingly — forgive all of this. Victory, victory to you, ocean of compassion! O great, auspicious Lord Shiva!

— Shiva Aparadha Kshamapana Stotram (Adi Shankaracharya)
