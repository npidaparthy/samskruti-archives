parokṣe yasya durvṛttaṃ pratyakṣe priyasambhāṣī ।
varjayedvyāghravalloke guḍāṅgāra ivānalam ॥

One who behaves wickedly behind your back yet speaks pleasantly to your face — such a person should be shunned like a tiger, like fire hidden beneath a coating of sugar.

— Subhashita tradition (echoing Chanakya's mitra-lakshana)
