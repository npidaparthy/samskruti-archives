yajante sāttvikā devānyakṣarakṣāṃsi rājasāḥ ।
pretānbhūtagaṇāṃścānye yajante tāmasā janāḥ ॥

The sattvic worship the gods; the rajasic worship the yakshas and demonic spirits; the tamasic worship ghosts and hordes of spirits.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Śraddhātraya Vibhāga Yoga)
