tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau ।
jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi ॥

Therefore, in determining what should and should not be done, scripture is your authority. Understanding what is enjoined by scriptural teaching, you should perform action accordingly.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Daivāsura Sampad Vibhāga Yoga)
