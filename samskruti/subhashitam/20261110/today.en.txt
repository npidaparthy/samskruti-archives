parasya yoṣitsamavāptasampado
dvitīyarūpeṇa ca dṛṣṭibhājanam ।
yayaiva lokasya mano'bhiramyate
tayaiva cittaṃ pratidūyate mama ॥

Another's acquired wealth, and — in another form — casting one's gaze at another's spouse: the very things by which the world's mind finds delight are the very things by which my own mind burns with torment.

— Subhashita Ratnabhandagara
