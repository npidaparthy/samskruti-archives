brahmabhūtaḥ prasannātmā na śocati na kāṅkṣati ।
samaḥ sarveṣu bhūteṣu madbhaktiṃ labhate parām ॥

One who has become one with Brahman, of tranquil mind, neither grieves nor craves. Equal toward all beings, he attains supreme devotion to me.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
