dehabuddhyā tu dāso'haṃ jīvabuddhyā tvadaṃśakaḥ ।
ātmabuddhyā tvamevāhamiti me niścitā matiḥ ॥

By the reckoning of the body, I am your servant; by the reckoning of the individual soul, I am a part of you; by the reckoning of the Self, you alone are I — this is my settled conviction.

— Attributed to Hanuman (bhakti tradition)
