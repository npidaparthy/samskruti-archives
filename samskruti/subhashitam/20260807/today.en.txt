maccittā madgataprāṇā bodhayantaḥ parasparam ।
kathayantaśca māṃ nityaṃ tuṣyanti ca ramanti ca ॥

Devotees whose mind and very breath are fixed on me, enlightening one another and constantly speaking of me, find contentment and delight.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Vibhūti Yoga)
