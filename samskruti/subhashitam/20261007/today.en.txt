guṇā guṇajñeṣu guṇā bhavanti
te nirguṇaṃ prāpya bhavanti doṣāḥ ।
āsvādyatoyāḥ prabhavanti nadyaḥ
samudramāsādya bhavantyapeyāḥ ॥

Virtues remain virtues among those who can recognise them; in the company of the unworthy, the same virtues turn into faults. Rivers rise with sweet water — but on merging into the ocean, they turn undrinkable.

— Hitopadesha (Mitralabha)
