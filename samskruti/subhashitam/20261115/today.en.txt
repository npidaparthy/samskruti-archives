tathā caturvidhaṃ vācastapaḥ paramamucyate ।
anudvegakaraṃ vākyaṃ satyaṃ priyahitaṃ ca yat ।
svādhyāyābhyasanaṃ caiva vāṅmayaṃ tapa ucyate ॥

Speech that causes no agitation, that is truthful, that is both pleasant and beneficial, together with regular study and recitation — this fourfold discipline of speech is called the highest austerity.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Śraddhātraya Vibhāga Yoga)
