dūreṇa hyavaraṃ karma buddhiyogāddhanañjaya ।
buddhau śaraṇamanviccha kṛpaṇāḥ phalahetavaḥ ॥

O Dhananjaya! Mere action, compared to the yoga of equanimity, is far inferior. Seek refuge in that equanimity — those who act driven by desire for results are pitiable.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
