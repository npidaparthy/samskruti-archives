sadṛśaṃ ceṣṭate svasyāḥ prakṛterjñānavānapi ।
prakṛtiṃ yānti bhūtāni nigrahaḥ kiṃ kariṣyati ॥

Even the man of knowledge acts according to his own nature. All beings follow their own nature — what, then, can forcible suppression accomplish?

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Karma Yoga)
