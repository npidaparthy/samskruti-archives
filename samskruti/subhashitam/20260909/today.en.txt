yataḥ pravṛttirbhūtānāṃ yena sarvamidaṃ tatam ।
svakarmaṇā tamabhyarcya siddhiṃ vindati mānavaḥ ॥

He from whom all beings have their origin, by whom all this is pervaded — worshipping Him through one's own work, a person attains perfection.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
