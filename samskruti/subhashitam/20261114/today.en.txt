āśā nāma nadī manorathajalā tṛṣṇātaraṅgākulā
rāgagrāhavatī vitarkavihagā dhairyadrumadhvaṃsinī ।
mohāvartasudustarātigahanā prottuṅgacintātaṭī
tasyāḥ pāragatā viśuddhamanaso nandanti yogīśvarāḥ ॥

There is a river called Hope, whose water is desire, whose waves are craving, infested with the crocodile of passion, whose birds are doubt; it sweeps away the trees of courage, is nearly impossible to cross for its whirlpools of delusion, and its banks are towering worries. Only those pure-minded lords of yoga who have crossed to its far shore find true joy.

— Bhartrhari's Vairagya Shataka
