mātā śatruḥ pitā vairī yena bālo na pāṭhitaḥ ।
na śobhate sabhāmadhye haṃsamadhye bako yathā ॥

A mother who does not have her child educated is his enemy; a father who neglects it is his foe. Such a person does not shine in an assembly — as a crane does not shine among swans.

— Chanakya Niti
