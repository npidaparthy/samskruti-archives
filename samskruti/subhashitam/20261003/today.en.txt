abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhirjñānayogavyavasthitiḥ ।
dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam ॥

Fearlessness, purity of being, steadfastness in the yoga of knowledge, charity, self-restraint, sacrifice, self-study, austerity, and uprightness — these are the marks of divine endowment.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Daivāsura Sampad Vibhāga Yoga)
