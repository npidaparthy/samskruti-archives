ārabhyate na khalu vighnabhayena nīcaiḥ
prārabhya vighnavihatā viramanti madhyāḥ ।
vighnaiḥ punaḥ punarapi pratihanyamānāḥ
prārabdhamuttamajanā na parityajanti ॥

The lowly, from fear of obstacles, do not even begin; the middling begin but stop the moment obstacles strike; the excellent, though struck by obstacles again and again, never abandon what they have undertaken.

— Niti Shataka tradition (widely quoted)
