yogasthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṃ tyaktvā dhanañjaya ।
siddhyasiddhyoḥ samo bhūtvā samatvaṃ yoga ucyate ॥

O Dhananjaya! Abandoning attachment, equal alike in success and failure, perform your actions established in yoga. That evenness of mind is what is called yoga.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
