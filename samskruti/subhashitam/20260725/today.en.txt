kākaceṣṭā bakodhyānaṃ śvānanidrā tathaiva ca ।
alpāhārī gṛhatyāgī vidyārthī pañcalakṣaṇam ॥

The alertness of a crow, the single-pointed focus of a crane, the light sleep of a dog, moderation in food, and readiness to leave the comfort of home — these are the five marks of a true student.

— Chanakya Niti
