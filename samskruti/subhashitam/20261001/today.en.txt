udyoginaṃ puruṣasiṃhamupaiti lakṣmīḥ
daivena deyamiti kāpuruṣā vadanti ।
daivaṃ nihatya kuru pauruṣamātmaśaktyā
yatne kṛte yadi na sidhyati ko'tra doṣaḥ ॥

Fortune comes only to the industrious lion among men. 'Fate will provide,' say the timid. Set fate aside and strive with your own strength. If, after honest effort, success still eludes you — what blame is there in that?

— Panchatantra (Mitralabha)
