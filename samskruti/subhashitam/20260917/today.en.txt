ayamātmā nadītīre yathā vṛkṣo na tiṣṭhati ।
patantamupakārāya tathā jīvitamātmanaḥ ॥

A tree on a riverbank does not stand forever — it is bound to fall someday. So too, before this bodily life falls away, it should be spent in the service of others.

— Subhashita tradition
