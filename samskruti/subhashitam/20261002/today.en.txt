kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā buddhyātmanā vā prakṛteḥ svabhāvāt ।
karomi yadyat sakalaṃ parasmai nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayāmi ॥

Whatever I do through body, speech, mind, senses, intellect, or soul — or by the inherent nature of my being — all of it in its entirety I offer to Nārāyaṇa, the Supreme.

— Nārāyaṇa Samarpaṇa — devotional tradition
