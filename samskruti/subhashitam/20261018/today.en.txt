na corahāryaṃ na ca rājahāryaṃ na bhrātṛbhājyaṃ na ca bhārakāri ।
vyaye kṛte vardhata eva nityaṃ vidyādhanaṃ sarvadhanapradhānam ॥

The wealth of knowledge cannot be stolen by thieves, taken by kings, divided among siblings, or felt as a burden; the more it is shared, the more it grows — therefore it is the foremost among all wealth.

— Hitopadeśa
