klaibyaṃ mā sma gamaḥ pārtha naitattvayyupapadyate ।
kṣudraṃ hṛdayadaurbalyaṃ tyaktvottiṣṭha parantapa ॥

O Partha! Do not yield to this weakness — it does not befit you. Cast off this petty faintheartedness, and arise, O scorcher of enemies!

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Sāṅkhya Yoga)
