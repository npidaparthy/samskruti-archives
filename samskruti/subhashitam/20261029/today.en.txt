yatra yogeśvaraḥ kṛṣṇo yatra pārtho dhanurdharaḥ ।
tatra śrīrvijayo bhūtirdhruvā nītirmatirmama ॥

Where Kṛṣṇa, the lord of yoga, is; where Arjuna, the bowman, is — there, I am convinced, will be glory, victory, prosperity, and unwavering righteousness.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Mokṣa Sannyāsa Yoga)
