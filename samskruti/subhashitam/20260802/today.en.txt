ananyacetāḥ satataṃ yo māṃ smarati nityaśaḥ ।
tasyāhaṃ sulabhaḥ pārtha nityayuktasya yoginaḥ ॥

For the yogi who, with undivided mind, remembers me constantly and unceasingly — O Partha — for that ever-united yogi, I am easily attained.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Akṣara Brahma Yoga)
