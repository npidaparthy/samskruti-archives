yāvadvittopārjanasaktaḥ
tāvannijaparivāro raktaḥ ।
paścājjīvati jarjaradehe
vārtāṃ ko'pi na pṛcchati gehe ॥

So long as a man is busy earning wealth, his own family is devoted to him. Afterward, when he lives on with a frail, worn body, no one in the house even asks how he is.

— Bhaja Govindam (Adi Shankaracharya)
