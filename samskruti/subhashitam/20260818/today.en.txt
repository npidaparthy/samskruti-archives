śraddhayā deyam । aśraddhayā'deyam ।
śriyā deyam । hriyā deyam ।
bhiyā deyam । saṃvidā deyam ॥

Give with faith; never give without faith. Give according to your means; give with modesty; give with reverence; give with understanding and fellow-feeling.

— Taittiriya Upanishad (Shiksha Valli)
