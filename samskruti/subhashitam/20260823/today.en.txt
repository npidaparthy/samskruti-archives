punarapi jananaṃ punarapi maraṇaṃ punarapi jananījaṭhare śayanam ।
iha saṃsāre bahudustāre kṛpayāpāre pāhi murāre ॥

Again birth, again death, again lying in the mother's womb — in this cycle of existence so hard to cross, protect me, O Murāri (Kṛṣṇa), with your boundless compassion.

— Bhaja Govindam — Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (verse 21)
