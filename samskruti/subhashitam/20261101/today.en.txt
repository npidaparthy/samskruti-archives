cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśaḥ ।
tasya kartāramapi māṃ viddhyakartāramavyayam ॥

According to the division of quality and action, I created the four-fold order. Though I am its creator, know me as the non-doer, the unchanging one.

— Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Jñāna Karma Sannyāsa Yoga)
