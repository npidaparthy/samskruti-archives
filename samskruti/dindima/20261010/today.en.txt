cintyācintyapadārthau dvau viśrāntiśrāntidāyakau ।
cintyaṃ brahma paraṃ nānyaditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 9 ॥

What is worth contemplating and what is not are two categories, giving repose and exhaustion respectively. What is truly worth contemplating is the Supreme Brahman alone, nothing else. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
