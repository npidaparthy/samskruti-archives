na satyaḥ kathyate loko nāmādervyabhicārataḥ ।
vaṭuḥ kulaṭa ityāderiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 69 ॥

Since designations such as 'boy' or 'unmarried girl' keep changing with time, one cannot call the person so described truly real, based on such names. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
