dehādikośagā sattā yā sā vyomādibhūtagā ।
mānābhāvānna tadbheda iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 57 ॥

The existence found within the sheaths and the existence found within the elements are one and the same. Since no evidence can establish such a difference, there is none. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
