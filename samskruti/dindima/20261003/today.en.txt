deho nāhamahaṃ dehī dehasākṣīti niścayāt ।
janmamṛtyuprahīṇo'sāviti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 43 ॥

The body is not I; I am the indweller of the body, the witness of the body — through this firm conviction, that person becomes free from birth and death. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
