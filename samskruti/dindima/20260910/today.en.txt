śrotavyāśrāvyarūpau dvau padārthau sukhaduḥkhadau ।
śrotavyaṃ brahma naivānyaditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 8 ॥

What is worth hearing and what is not are two categories, giving joy and sorrow respectively. What is truly worth hearing is Brahman alone, nothing else. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
