yannādau yacca nāstyante tanmadhye bhātamapyasat ।
ato mithyā jagatsarvamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 37 ॥

Whatever does not exist at the beginning and does not exist at the end, though it may appear in between, is unreal. Therefore the entire universe is unreal. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
