ṣaṇnihatya trayaṃ hitvā dvayaṃ bhittvā'khilātigam ।
ekaṃ buddhvā'śnute mokṣamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 41 ॥

Having destroyed the six inner enemies, given up the three gunas, pierced through the all-pervading duality, and realised the one — such a person attains liberation. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
