sukhamalpaṃ bahukleśaṃ viṣayagrāhiṇāṃ nṛṇām ।
anantaṃ brahmaniṣṭhānāmiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 77 ॥

For people who chase after sense-objects, pleasure is small and trouble abundant. For those established in Brahman, joy is boundless. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
