navabhyo'sti paraḥ pratyaṅnaiva veda paraṃ param ।
tadvijñānādbhavettulyā muktirvedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 82 ॥

There exists, beyond the nine, an inner supreme reality; that supreme itself does not know itself until it is inquired into. Upon knowing it, an equivalent liberation arises. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
