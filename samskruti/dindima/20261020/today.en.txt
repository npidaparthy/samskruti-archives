saccidānandarūpatvādbrahmaivātmā na saṃśayaḥ ।
śrutipramānusandhānāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 60 ॥

Owing to its being of the nature of existence-consciousness-bliss, and confirmed through the investigation of Vedic testimony, the Self is Brahman alone — there is no doubt. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
