puruṣārthatrayāviṣṭāḥ puruṣāḥ paśavo dhruvam ।
mokṣārthī puruṣaśreṣṭha iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 39 ॥

Those absorbed in the three worldly aims of dharma, artha, and kama are certainly no better than beasts. Only the seeker of liberation is truly the foremost among men. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
