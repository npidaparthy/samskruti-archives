naivābhāsaṃ spṛśetkarma mithyopādhimapi svayam ।
kuto'dhiṣṭhānamatyacchamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 92 ॥

Action does not even touch the mere reflection cast upon an unreal limiting adjunct — how then could it possibly touch the utterly pure substratum itself? This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
