na jīvabrahmaṇorbhedaḥ piṇḍabrahmāṇḍabhedataḥ ।
vyaṣṭeḥ samaṣṭerekatvāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 66 ॥

The apparent difference between the individual body and the cosmic body does not establish any real difference between jiva and Brahman — for the individual and the collective whole are ultimately one. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
