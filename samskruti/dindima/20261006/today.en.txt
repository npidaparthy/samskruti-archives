na jīvabrahmaṇorbhedaḥ sphūrtirūpeṇa vidyate ।
sphūrtibhede na mānaṃ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 63 ॥

There is no difference between the individual and Brahman with respect to luminous awareness. No proof can be given for such a difference. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
