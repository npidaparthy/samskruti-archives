ātmānātmapadārthau dvau bhoktṛbhogyatvalakṣaṇau ।
brahmaivātmā na dehādiriti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 2 ॥

The self and the non-self are two categories — marked by the nature of the enjoyer and the enjoyed. Of these, the self is Brahman itself, not the body and so on. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
