advaitadvaitavādau dvau sūkṣmasthūladaśāṃ gatau ।
advaitavādānmokṣaḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 19 ॥

Non-dualism and dualism are two doctrines — belonging to the subtle and gross levels respectively. Liberation comes from the doctrine of non-duality. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
