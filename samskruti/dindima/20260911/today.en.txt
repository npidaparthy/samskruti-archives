vyomādipañcabhūtasthā yā sattā bhāsate nṛṇām ।
sā sattā paramaṃ brahma iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 54 ॥

The existence that shines forth for human beings within the five elements, beginning with space, is the Supreme Brahman itself. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
