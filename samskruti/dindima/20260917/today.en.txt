vedāntabherījhaṅkāraḥ prativādibhayaṅkaraḥ ।
śrūyatāṃ brāhmaṇaiḥ śrīmaddakṣiṇāmūrtyanugrahāt ॥ 94 ॥
॥ oṃ tat sat ॥

Let this resounding drumbeat of Vedanta, which strikes fear into opposing disputants, be heard by seekers of Brahman, through the grace of the glorious Lord Dakshinamurti.

— Vedanta Dindimah
