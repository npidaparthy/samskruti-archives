na matābhiniveśena na bhāṣāveṣamātrataḥ ।
muktirvinātmavijñānānamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 72 ॥

Liberation does not come through fanatical attachment to a doctrine, nor through mere language or outward appearance. Without knowledge of the Self, there is no liberation. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
