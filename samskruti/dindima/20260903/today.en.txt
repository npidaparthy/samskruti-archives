na jīvabrahmaṇorbhedaḥ sattārūpeṇa vidyate ।
sattābhede na mānaṃ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 62 ॥

There is no difference between the individual and Brahman with respect to existence. No proof can be given for such a difference. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
