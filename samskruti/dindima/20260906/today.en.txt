samaṣṭivyaṣṭirūpau dvau padārthau sarvasammatau ।
samaṣṭirīśvaro vyaṣṭirjīvo vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 6 ॥

The collective whole and the individual part are two categories, accepted by all thinkers. The collective whole is Īśvara; the individual part is the jīva. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
