ahaṃ sākṣīti yo vidyādvivicyaivaṃ punaḥ punaḥ ।
sa eva mukto'sau vidvāniti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 48 ॥

Whoever, discriminating thus, comes to know again and again 'I am the witness' — that very learned one alone is liberated. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
