sañcitāgāmikarmāṇi dahyante jñānavahninā ।
prārabdhānyapyanubhavāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 27 ॥

Accumulated and future karmas are burnt away by the fire of knowledge. Prarabdha karma alone is exhausted only through experience. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
