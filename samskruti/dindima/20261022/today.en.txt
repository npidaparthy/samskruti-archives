na kuryānna vijānīyātsarvaṃ brahmetyanusmaran ।
yathā sukhaṃ tathā tiṣṭhediti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 89 ॥

Constantly recollecting that everything is Brahman, one need not particularly act nor particularly seek to know anything further; one may simply remain in whatever way is comfortable. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
