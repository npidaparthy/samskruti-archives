avijñāte janma naṣṭaṃ vijñāte janma sārthakam ।
jñāturātmā na dūre syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 74 ॥

If the Self remains unknown, one's birth is wasted; if it becomes known, one's birth is fulfilled. For the one who knows, the Self is never far away. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
