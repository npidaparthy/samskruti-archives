sālambanaṃ nirālambaṃ sarvālambāvalambitam ।
avalambenākhilālamba iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 88 ॥

Brahman is both that which is supported and that which is without support — every support ultimately rests upon it. It is precisely through its own independence from support that it becomes the support of all. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
