na varṇāśramasaṅketairna karmopāsanādibhiḥ ।
brahmajñānaṃ vinā mokṣa iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 12 ॥

Liberation does not come through the markers of caste and life-stage, nor through rituals and meditations. Without knowledge of Brahman, there is no liberation. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
