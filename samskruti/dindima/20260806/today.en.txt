brahma satyaṃ jaganmithyā jīvo brahmaiva nāparaḥ ।
jīvanmuktastu tadvidvāniti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 67 ॥

Brahman is real, the world is unreal, the individual is Brahman and nothing else. One who knows this is liberated while yet living. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
