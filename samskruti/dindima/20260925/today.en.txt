dehādikośagā prītiryā sā vyomādibhūtagā ।
mānābhāvānna tadbheda iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 59 ॥

The joy within the sheaths and the joy within the elements are one and the same. Since no difference can be established, there is none. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
