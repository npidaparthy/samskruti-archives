aihikaṃ cāmuṣmikaṃ ca tāpāntaṃ karmasañcayam ।
tyaktvā brahmaiva vijñeyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 18 ॥

Abandoning the accumulation of actions — pursued for this world and the next, and ending only in suffering — one should strive to know Brahman alone. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
