buddhirnāhamahaṃ devo buddhisākṣīti niścayāt ।
kartṛbhāvanivṛttiḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 46 ॥

The intellect is not I; I am self-luminous, the witness of the intellect — through this firm conviction, the sense of doership ceases. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
