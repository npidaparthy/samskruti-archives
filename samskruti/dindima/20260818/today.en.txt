sattāsphuraṇasaukhyāni bhāsante sarvavastuṣu ।
tasmādbrahmamayaṃ sarvamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 35 ॥

Existence, luminous awareness, and felicity shine forth in every single object. Therefore everything is pervaded by Brahman. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
