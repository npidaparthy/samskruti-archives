mano nāhamahaṃ devo manaḥsākṣīti niścayāt ।
śokamohāpahāniḥ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 45 ॥

The mind is not I; I am self-luminous, the witness of the mind — through this firm conviction, sorrow and delusion are dispelled. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
