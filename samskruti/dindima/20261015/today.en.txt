pañcānāmapi kośānāṃ māyānarthātmatocitā ।
tatsākṣibrahmavijñānānāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 80 ॥

It is fitting that all five sheaths are of the nature of maya, transient and without true significance. Knowing the witness-Brahman of those sheaths alone is true knowledge. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
