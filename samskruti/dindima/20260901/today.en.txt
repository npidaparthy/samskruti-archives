sukhaduḥkhapadārthau dvau priyavipriyakārakau ।
sukhaṃ brahma jagadduḥkhamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 5 ॥

Joy and sorrow are two categories — one bringing what is pleasant, the other what is unpleasant. Joy is Brahman itself; the universe is sorrow. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
