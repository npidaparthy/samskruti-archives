bhittvā ṣaṭ pañca bhittvātha gatvāgre caturastrikam ।
dvayaṃ hitvāśrayedekamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 42 ॥

Piercing through the six, piercing through the five, then advancing past the four, and giving up the two — one should take refuge in the one alone. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
