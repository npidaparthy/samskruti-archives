avasthātritayaṃ yasya krīḍābhūmitayā sthitam ।
tadeva brahma jānīyāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 36 ॥

Know as Brahman that consciousness for which the three states — waking, dream, and deep sleep — exist merely as a playground. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
