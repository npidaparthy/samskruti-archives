na kāmyapratiṣiddhābhiḥ kriyābhirmokṣavāsanā ।
īśvarānugrahātsā syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 73 ॥

The yearning for liberation does not arise through desire-driven actions, nor through avoiding forbidden ones. It comes only through the grace of the Lord. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
