narcā na yajuṣārtho'sti na sāmnārtho'sti kaścana ।
jāte brahmātmavijñāna iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 25 ॥

Once knowledge of Brahman as the self has arisen, there is no purpose served by the Rigveda, none by the Yajurveda, and none whatsoever by the Samaveda. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
