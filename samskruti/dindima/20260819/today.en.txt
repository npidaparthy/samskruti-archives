asatyaḥ sarvasaṃsāro rasābhāsādidūṣitaḥ ।
upekṣyo brahma vijñeyamiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 13 ॥

The entirety of worldly existence is unreal, tainted with the mere semblance of true pleasure. One should treat it with indifference and strive to know Brahman. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
