na jīvabrahmaṇorbhedaḥ priyarūpeṇa vidyate ।
priyabhede na mānaṃ syāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 64 ॥

There is no difference between the individual and Brahman with respect to what is dear, that is, joy. No proof can be given for such a difference. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
