na puṇyakarmaṇo vṛddhirna hāniḥ pāpakarmaṇaḥ ।
nityāsaṅgātmaniṣṭhānāmiti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 28 ॥

For one who is ever established, unattached, in the Self, there is neither increase from virtuous action nor loss from sinful action. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
