nāmarūpātmakaṃ viśvamindrajālaṃ vidurbudhāḥ ।
anāmatvādarūpatvāditi vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 70 ॥

The wise recognise this universe, composed of name and form, to be a magic show — because it truly has no permanent name or form of its own. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
