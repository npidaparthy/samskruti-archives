daśamasya parijñānānnavajñasya yathā sukham ।
tathā jīvasya satprāptiriti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 81 ॥

Just as one who already knew nine attains joy on knowing the tenth as well, so too the individual attains true reality on knowing their own true nature. This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
