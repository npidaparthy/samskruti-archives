anāmarūpaṃ sakalaṃ sanmayaṃ cinmayaṃ priyam ।
kuto bhedaḥ kuto bandha iti vedāntaḍiṇḍimaḥ ॥ 68 ॥

Everything is beyond name and form, composed of existence, of consciousness, of bliss. Whence, then, could difference arise? Whence could bondage arise? This is the drumbeat of Vedanta.

— Vedanta Dindimah
