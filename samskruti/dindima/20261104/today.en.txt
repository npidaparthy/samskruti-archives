aho'smākamalaṃ mohairātma brahmeti nirbhayam ।
śrutibherīravo'dyāpi śrūyate śrutirañjanaḥ ॥ 93 ॥

Oh, what a wonder! Enough of our delusions! The fearless drumbeat sound of the scriptures, declaring 'the Self is Brahman', delightful to hear, is heard even to this day!

— Vedanta Dindimah
